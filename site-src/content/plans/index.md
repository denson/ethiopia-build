## Notes for agents on this chapter

- Plans are intentions, dated. A plan's "state" cell is the only place its status lives; the plan page's body is the plan as written plus dated amendments. Do not infer that a planned step has happened from its presence here.
- New plans are added at the bottom of the table. A plan that is abandoned stays listed with its state set to abandoned and the reason.
